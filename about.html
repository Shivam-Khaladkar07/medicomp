Our Story

MediComp was founded in 2024 by a team of healthcare professionals and technology experts who experienced firsthand the frustration of unclear medication pricing. After spending hours calling different pharmacies to find the best prices for family members' prescriptions, they realized there had to be a better way.

What started as a simple idea to help friends and family has grown into a comprehensive platform that serves thousands of users across the country. We've partnered with over 50 licensed pharmacies to provide real-time pricing information and help people save money on their essential medications.

Today, MediComp continues to evolve, adding new features and partnerships to better serve our community's healthcare needs.
